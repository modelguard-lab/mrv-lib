\section{Methodology}

For each asset, the model is fitted independently under every admissible
specification. Pairwise agreement between the resulting label assignments is
measured with the Adjusted Rand Index (ARI).
%% IF_IS_REP
Risk-ordering stability is
measured with a rank-aligned Spearman correlation against a risk proxy.
%% ENDIF

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Partition layer:} mean off-diagonal ARI across specification
      pairs. A value $\geq$ {{ARI_THRESHOLD}} indicates acceptable partition
      recovery (Steinley, 2004).
%% IF_IS_REP
\item \textbf{Ordering layer:} mean Spearman correlation across specification
      pairs. A value $\geq$ {{SPEARMAN_THRESHOLD}} indicates a stable ordinal
      risk ranking even when categorical labels differ.
%% ENDIF
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
Theoretical basis: Zheng, Low \& Wang (2026), representation invariance
(Paper 1) and resolution invariance (Paper 2).
